Ficus benghalensis

Ficus benghalensis, Ficus indica, or Ficus audrey, commonly known as banyan, banyan fig, audrey fig and Indian banyan, is a tree native to the Indian subcontinent. It is an evergreen, monoecious, fast-growing tree found mainly in monsoon forests and rainforests.
