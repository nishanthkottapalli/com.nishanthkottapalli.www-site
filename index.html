Ficus benghalensis

Ficus benghalensis, Ficus indica, or Ficus audrey, commonly known as banyan, banyan fig, audrey fig and Indian banyan, is a tree native to the Indian subcontinent. It is an evergreen, monoecious, fast-growing tree found mainly in monsoon forests and rainforests.

Knowledge tree

Ground captures reasoning, observation and evidence. Roots are broad source domains. Trunks are stable bodies of knowledge. Branches are specialised or applied capabilities. Leaves are the artefacts that can be read, played, reused or inspected.

Knowledge graph

A leaf can connect to several branches, trunks, ventures, technologies and sources. The tree explains where something appears; the graph explains what it is, what it demonstrates and how it relates to the rest of the ecosystem.
