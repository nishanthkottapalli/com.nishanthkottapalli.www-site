Ex nihilo nihil fit

Branches are the specialised topics, practices and capabilities through which a stable body of knowledge becomes useful. They narrow a Trunk into something that can be studied, applied, demonstrated and connected to inspectable Leaves.

The canonical model 

Ground — reasoning, observation, questions, claims, evidence and decisions
└── Roots — broad source domains and enduring interests
    └── Trunks — coherent bodies of knowledge
        └── Branches — specialised topics, practices and capabilities
            └── Leaves — projects, repositories, writing, experiments and other artefacts

Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science and Electronics are not treated as peer Branches here. They are broad foundational bodies of knowledge, or parts of broader foundational Trunks. Their narrower areas—such as probability, distributed systems, electromagnetism or embedded systems—behave as Branches.

What can be a Branch?

Specialised topic

A narrower area of study within a Trunk, such as Distributed Systems, Game AI or Data Protection.

Practice

A repeatable way of working, such as Threat Modelling, Experiment Design or Content Operations.

Capability

An applied ability that can be demonstrated, offered or reused, such as Platform Engineering or Detection Engineering.

Technique

A focused method such as Frequency Capping, Entity Resolution, Procedural Generation or Infrastructure Scanning.

Branch families

The catalogue is grouped by parent Trunk for navigation. In the underlying knowledge graph, a Branch may belong to more than one Trunk and a Leaf may demonstrate several Branches at once.

Minimum data for a published Branch

Identity

Stable identifier, canonical label, definition, lifecycle status and last-reviewed date.

Context

Parent Trunk or Trunks, associated Roots, related Branches and narrower techniques.

Evidence

Representative Leaves, repositories, writing, experiments, projects, products or case studies.

Ecosystem

People, organisations, ventures, brands, services or products that develop, apply, support or commercialise the capability.
